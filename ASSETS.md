# Assets

The architectural raster assets were generated for this workspace with the built-in ImageGen tool. They depict illustrative concepts and should be labeled accordingly.

- `public/images/architecture/courtyard.webp` and `courtyard-small.webp` share the prompt “Architectural editorial photograph of a contemporary courtyard residence in Indore with warm terracotta board-formed concrete, stone plinth, deep cantilevers and native planting at dusk.”
- `public/images/properties/apartment.webp`, `villa.webp`, `office.webp` and `public/images/interiors/living.webp` were cropped from the prompt “2 by 2 photographic contact sheet of contemporary Indian architecture with an Indore apartment exterior, independent courtyard villa, sunlit Indian living room and modern commercial office facade.”
- The plot drawing, favicon and in-app floor plans are original SVG assets. The architectural concept gallery uses independent studies.
- Playfair Display, Manrope, IBM Plex Mono and Noto Sans Devanagari are served locally through Fontsource packages under their respective SIL Open Font Licenses. The app includes system fallback fonts.
- OpenStreetMap contributors supply basemap data under ODbL. The attribution is displayed on the interactive map.
